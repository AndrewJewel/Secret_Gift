Questions

Can the organizer see who got whom?

No. Nobody can. Each person sees only their own result, protected by a PIN. In the chat, everyone writes behind a mask.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It works in any browser on your phone or computer, and there's an app for Android.

What if someone can't take part anymore?

The organizer can replace them, even after the draw. The new person takes their place and nobody else's result changes.
